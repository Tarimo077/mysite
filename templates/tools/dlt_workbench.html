Important encoding rules

645 DATA bytes are encoded by adding 0x33 before transmission and decoded by subtracting 0x33. The checksum is the modulo-256 sum from the first 68 through the final DATA byte. The 6-byte address is BCD and transmitted low-byte first.
This tool follows the supplied meter-development and MQTT protocol documents. Where the documents do not define a numeric scale or semantic format, the UI intentionally uses exact-width raw bytes instead of guessing.
